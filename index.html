What happens at NMMUN

For two days the Shirpur campus turns into the United Nations. You are given a country, a committee and a problem the real world has not solved yet.

You research your country's position, speak for it on the floor, negotiate in the corridors and write a resolution that other delegates are willing to sign. It is run entirely by students of NMIMS Shirpur, and it is open to school and college students from across India, whether this is your first conference or your tenth.

Once you have your committee

  1. Read your country. Learn where it stands on the agenda, who its allies are and what it has voted for before.
  2. Write a position paper. One page: the problem, your country's view and the solution you will propose.
  3. Prepare a one-minute speech. Your opening statement sets the tone. Say what your country wants in plain words.
  4. Dress the part. Western or Indian formals. You are a diplomat for two days.
